# The hackathon demo video

**Delivered by:** a labeled team creator account.

Make the video that presents BONFIRE to the hackathon judges. It gets made only if this mission wins, and it opens by saying so.

**Deliver:**

- An MP4 of at most 3 minutes. Its first line says it was commissioned by BONFIRE Round 2 and how much EMBER burned to make it.
- It shows the live round, a commitment from a guest account on a phone, the candle, the Practice floor, a refused attack, and the Ledger's proof checks.
- Every transaction it shows as confirmed is a real one, with its hash on screen or in the description.
- A note naming the tools used to make it.

**Acceptance:** the reviewer accepts the exact submitted SHA-256 of the MP4, the file is in the repository, and none of it was published before this round's backing closed.
