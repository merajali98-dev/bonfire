# EMBER sticker pack

**Delivered by:** a labeled team creator account.

Make six new stickers of the EMBER mascot for people to use when they share the fire. They get made only if this mission wins. The mascot already exists as site art, and that art is not part of this mission.

**Deliver:**

- Six new stickers in SVG or PNG on transparent backgrounds, readable at 64 px.
- Each one shows the mascot in a pose or scene the site doesn't already use.
- A note saying which tools made them. AI image tools are allowed and must be named.
- Free for anyone to use.

**Acceptance:** the reviewer accepts the exact submitted SHA-256 of a zip holding all six files and the note, none of the stickers was published before this round's backing closed, and the stickers are on the site.
